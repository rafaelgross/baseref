# BaseRef pilot review (project 1): screening decisions, 11 November 2026

Read-only export of the votes stored in BaseRef for the pilot review "Blockchain and the circular economy" (BaseRef project 1), as recorded on 11 November 2026. Reviewers are identified by initials only: RG (reviewer 1, project owner) and RFG (reviewer 2). Dates and times are local time with the UTC offset. Exclusion reasons are stored in Portuguese, as entered in BaseRef.

| File | Rows | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `decisions_screening.csv` | 240 | Title and abstract votes of RG and RFG for every screened record, agreement flag and consensus decision when recorded. |
| `decisions_fulltext.csv` | 63 | Full-text votes of RG and RFG. `in_final_flow` = 1 for the 55 reports sought for retrieval; `*_not_retrieved` = 1 when the reason is "full text unavailable"; `in_kappa_matrix` = 1 for the 42 reports assessed by both reviewers and included by both at screening. |
| `consensus.csv` | 5 | Consensus decisions recorded in BaseRef (all at title and abstract screening). |
| `included_studies.csv` | 32 | Studies included after full-text reading: 29 with agreement of both reviewers and 3 (491, 543, 545) awaiting a full-text consensus decision. |
